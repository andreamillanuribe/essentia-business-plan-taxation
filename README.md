# ESSENTIA S.L. – Business Plan and Tax Procedures

## Project Overview
Academic project focused on developing a business plan for ESSENTIA S.L., a startup specialising in smart luxury jewellery.

The project combines business planning, financial forecasting and the study of Spanish tax procedures required for setting up and managing a limited liability company.

## Project Objectives
- Develop a comprehensive business plan.
- Analyse initial investment and start-up costs.
- Prepare financial forecasts and revenue projections.
- Identify the company's tax obligations in Spain.
- Study the procedures required to establish a Limited Liability Company (S.L.).
- Analyse the relevant tax forms and administrative procedures.

## Key Areas
- Business Planning
- Financial Analysis and Forecasting
- Start-up Costs and Funding
- Spanish Tax Procedures
- Corporate Tax
- VAT (IVA)
- Tax Registration (Modelo 036)
- Economic Activities Tax (IAE)
- Digital Certificates and Electronic Notifications
